About BloodConnect

BloodConnect is a third-year BCA college-project demonstration for connecting blood donors, users, hospitals and blood camps through a Flask and MySQL application.

The records shown by the application are database records entered into BloodConnect by participating users and hospitals.

{% elif page == 'how_it_works' %}

How BloodConnect Works

1.

A user searches blood or creates a request.

2.

Flask validates the form and stores the request in MySQL.

3.

The matching service checks compatible, verified and available donor records.

4.

Database notifications can be shown to matching donors.

5.

A donor can respond and an authorized hospital can confirm or complete the request.

{% elif page == 'donation_guide' %}

Blood Donation Guide

Before donation

Follow the blood donation centre's instructions, eat normally and stay hydrated unless the centre tells you otherwise.

During donation

Qualified blood donation staff collect the donation and monitor you during the process.

After donation

Follow the centre's recovery instructions and report any concerning symptoms to the staff.

This project provides general information only and is not a medical decision tool.

{% elif page == 'eligibility' %}

Eligibility Information

Eligibility depends on factors such as age, health, medications, recent procedures and local blood-donation rules.

BloodConnect does not make final eligibility decisions. Final eligibility should be determined by qualified medical professionals or blood donation staff at the donation site.

{% elif page == 'assistant' %}

Blood Donation Assistant

What is a blood group?

A blood group identifies characteristics of blood used for transfusion compatibility. BloodConnect supports A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+ and O- in this demo.

How do I find blood?

Use Find Blood and filter by blood group, city, area and donor availability.

How do I request blood?

After login, create a normal or emergency request. The request is saved in MySQL and can be tracked.

Who decides donor eligibility?

Qualified medical professionals and blood donation staff determine final eligibility.

{% elif page == 'faq' %}

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this show every blood bank?

No. It only shows participating hospitals and records entered into this project database.

Does BloodConnect reveal donor addresses?

No. Public matching uses city and area-level information.

Are emergency messages browser notifications?

No. The project uses database notifications shown after login.

Can I use this for real medical decisions?

No. It is a college-project demonstration, not a medical service.
